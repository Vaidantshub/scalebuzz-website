## What Actually Drives Up the Cost of a 3D Website?

Building in 3D isn't like normal web development where you drop an `<img>` tag onto a flexbox layout. A real 3D website runs a live graphics engine inside the visitor's browser.

Here are the four biggest factors that dictate your quote:

### 1. 3D Asset Creation vs. Pre-made Models
Are you providing finished `.glb` files from your industrial designer, or do we need to model, UV-unwrap, and texture your product from scratch in Blender? 
Custom 3D modeling alone can add ₹25,000 to ₹80,000 to a project depending on geometry complexity.

### 2. Draw Calls and Shader Code
Standard websites don't worry about GPU draw calls. In WebGL, if your scene has 200 separate meshes with unbatched materials, mid-range Android phones will stutter at 15 frames per second. Writing custom GLSL shaders and batching geometry requires specialized engineering hours.

### 3. Mobile Performance and Asset Compression
A raw 3D scene can easily hit 45MB. We use **Draco compression** and meshoptimizer pipelines to squash that same file down to under 2.5MB, loading assets progressively so the visitor sees content in sub-second time.

## Can AI Make 3D Websites in 2026?

A lot of clients ask us: *"Can't AI tools just build this now?"*

The short answer is: **AI can generate pieces, but it cannot assemble a production site.**

Tools like Meshy or Tripo can generate rough 3D models from text prompts, and Claude or ChatGPT can spit out basic Three.js setup scripts. But AI cannot:
- Optimize polygon topology to prevent mobile overheating.
- Configure WebGL context restoration when a phone switches browser tabs.
- Harmonize typography, brand voice, and responsive layout around a 3D canvas.
- Ensure your site passes Google's Core Web Vitals thresholds.

You can check Google's official guidance on [Core Web Vitals metrics](https://web.dev/articles/vitals) to see why speed metrics like INP (Interaction to Next Paint) and LCP (Largest Contentful Paint) directly impact whether your site ranks on page 1.

## Is a 3D Website Right for Your Business?

Before spending your hard-earned capital, ask yourself what your primary business goal is:

### You SHOULD invest in a 3D website if:
- **You sell physical high-ticket products** (luxury furniture, electronics, custom jewelry, automotive accessories) where buyers want to inspect every angle before buying.
- **You are a tech, gaming, or Web3 company** looking to signal engineering caliber to global enterprise clients and investors.
- **You are an architecture, interior design, or exhibition studio** (like our client project DD by Sardanas) whose physical work demands spatial presentation.

### You SHOULD NOT invest in a 3D website if:
- You need a simple local service website (dentist, accountant, emergency plumber).
- Your marketing relies on high-velocity paid ad campaigns where every extra 200ms of page load cuts landing page conversions.
- Your budget is under ₹35,000. In that case, invest in a clean, lightning-fast 2D custom website with sharp typography instead.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### How long does it take to develop a custom 3D website in India?
Most 3D websites take between 3 and 7 weeks from discovery to production launch. Simple 3D hero sections take around 2 to 3 weeks, while custom interactive platforms with multiple 3D models require 5 to 8 weeks.

### Can 3D websites work smoothly on mobile phones in India?
Yes, provided the developer optimizes the WebGL pipeline. By reducing vertex counts, using compressed textures, and limiting post-processing bloom effects on mobile screens, 3D websites run smoothly at 60fps even on budget smartphones.

### How do I update text and products on a 3D website later?
We decouple the 3D scene from the content layer. Your copy, pricing, testimonials, and blog posts can be managed via a headless CMS or simple markdown files without ever touching the 3D canvas code.

### Do 3D websites cost more to host each month?
Not necessarily. Because 3D assets are static files (`.glb`, textures, WebAssembly loaders), they can be cached globally on edge CDNs like Vercel or Cloudflare. Standard hosting costs remain virtually identical to regular websites (often free to ₹1,500/month for moderate traffic).

### What assets do I need to provide to get started?
Ideally, your brand guidelines, logo files in vector format, vector copy, and any existing CAD or 3D files of your products. If you don't have 3D models, we can model them directly in Blender from product photos and dimension drawings.
